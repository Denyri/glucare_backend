## 1. Ringkasan Metodologi Knowledge Base

### Landasan Ilmiah Primer
Seluruh kriteria klinis dan ambang batas intervensi merujuk pada konsensus resmi:
1. **American Diabetes Association (ADA)**: *Standards of Care in Diabetes* (2024–2026), Chapter 2 (*Diagnosis*), Chapter 3 (*Prevention of T2D*), Chapter 5 (*Health Behaviors*), dan Chapter 10 (*Cardiovascular Risk*).
2. **CDC National Diabetes Prevention Program (National DPP)**: *Curriculum & Standards for Lifestyle Change Programs*.
3. **World Health Organization (WHO)**: *Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour* (2020) & *Healthy Diet Fact Sheet*.
4. **Diabetes Prevention Program Research Group (DPP)**: *Reduction in the Incidence of Type 2 Diabetes with Lifestyle Intervention or Metformin* (NEJM 2002).

## 3. Priority Scoring Framework

Rumus penentuan prioritas tindakan dirancang transparan:

$$\mathbf{Priority\ Score} = (\mathbf{CSS} \times 2) + \mathbf{BGS} + \mathbf{MRTM} + \mathbf{SRM}$$

* **Clinical Severity Score (CSS)**: Critical = 4, High = 3, Medium = 2, Low = 1.
* **Behavior Gap Score (BGS)**: Selisih $\ge 50\%$ dari target = 3, Selisih 20–49% = 2, Selisih $< 20\%$ = 1.
* **ML Risk Tier Modifier (MRTM)**: High Risk = +2, Moderate Risk = +1, Low Risk = 0.
* **Safety & Referral Modifier (SRM)**: Khusus aturan Healthcare Referral (HR) = +3.

Rentang skor berkisar antara **3 hingga 12**.

## 4. Personalization Algorithm & 90-Day Roadmap

### Hierarki Penanganan Konflik Aturan
$$\mathbf{Safety\ Rules\ (HR)} \succ \mathbf{Clinical\ Escalation\ (BP/WM)} \succ \mathbf{Behavior\ Modification\ (PA/NU)} \succ \mathbf{Gamification\ (XP/Streak)}$$

### Roadmap 4 Fase Program 90 Hari
1. **Fase 1 (Hari 1–7): Baseline & Awareness** — Membiasakan pencatatan log mandiri tanpa target ekstrem.
2. **Fase 2 (Hari 8–30): Stabilisasi Kebiasaan** — Penyelarasan ritme sirkadian dan defisit kalori moderat.
3. **Fase 3 (Hari 31–60): Optimasi Metabolik** — Target penuh 150 menit MVPA dan evaluasi Milestone Hari ke-30 (XGBoost Trajectory Model).
4. **Fase 4 (Hari 61–90): Konsolidasi & Retensi** — Menjaga keberlanjutan perubahan gaya hidup mandiri dan evaluasi akhir hasil intervensi.
